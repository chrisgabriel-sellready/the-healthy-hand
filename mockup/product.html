        Frequently asked questions

        Can I take Total Belly Detox and Total System Detox together?

        Yes, you can take both of our detoxes together. Our detox is amazing taken by itself, but together it is a powerhouse. If you take both, we suggest half a tablespoon of each, mixed together in a water bottle, as a tea, or added to a smoothie.

        How much water should I drink with the detox?

        It is best to drink at least 3 to 4 bottles of water per day after you have taken the detox. Water helps flush your system and lets the detox work properly. (Just water.)

        When is the best time to take the detox?

        The best time is before bed, or while you’re home or somewhere comfortable near a restroom. If you’re in the restroom more than normal, the product is working through your system. To slow it down, take a smaller dose.
