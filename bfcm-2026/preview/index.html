Why reactivate first

The list has been quiet for five months

Pulled from THH's Klaviyo account on 11/13. The newsletter list holds about 16,000 people, but it hasn't heard from us since June, and last year's BFCM went to the list cold.

Jun 21
Last email sent (Father's Day). That's 145 days of silence, and only 3 campaigns went out in all of 2026.
$745
BFCM 2025 revenue: 4 emails to about 13.5k people, 8 orders. Every send was a sale, and click rate sat near 0.5%.
0
SMS campaigns ever sent, even though there's an "SMS Subscribers" list. That channel hasn't been used yet.

What that means for this drop. If 16,000 people suddenly get mail during the busiest inbox week of the year, Gmail and Yahoo will start routing us to spam. So we warm up: Shay writes first to the people most likely to open, the list widens in waves, and only people who are engaging get the sale emails.

It also answers Alyssa's brief: reactivate the list, a message from Shay, the benefits of a detox, and the Thanksgiving-plate angle.

Built first, as Alyssa asked

THH brand kit

Built from THH's own logo, packaging and past emails, with the palette cut down to one accent. Every email, text and graphic in this drop follows it. Once you approve it, it can go into Klaviyo's brand library so new templates start on-brand.

Type

Detox, honestly.
Headlines: Fraunces (Georgia as the email fallback), 32 to 50px. Put one italic word in each headline for warmth.
One cup at night, two capsules in the morning. A small ritual makes a healthier week easier to keep.
Body: Poppins, already loaded in THH's Klaviyo. 16 to 18px, line height 1.6, left-aligned. No more centered text at 250% line height.
Balance due: 1 good reset
Utility: Courier Prime, only for borrowed formats like the receipt.

Voice

Warm, plain-spoken, rooted. We're a friend who knows herbs, not a pharmacy or a hype account.

  • Shay writes in first person and signs off "Shay."
  • Name the ingredient and the ritual: "soursop and ginger, steeped 5 minutes," not "natural wellness."
  • No more than one exclamation mark per email and one emoji per subject. None in buttons.
  • Urgency only with a real date and time, stated once.
  • Sender name every time: Shay from The Healthy Hand.

Buttons and layout

Shop the sale

  • Green, 6px radius, 2 to 4 words, verb first.
  • Header: Root bar (free shipping or the offer), the logo, then Detox · Bundles · Capsules · Gummies.
  • Headlines, offers and deadlines are always live text, never baked into images.
  • Footer: Root background, logo tile, the FDA disclaimer, unsubscribe and address.

Say this, not that

Supplements can make claims about how the body works, but not about disease or guaranteed weight loss. Every message in this drop follows this table.

Who gets what

Audience and guardrails

THH's Klaviyo has only suppression segments right now (bounces, can't receive, no email). These are the segments to create. The three existing suppression segments stay excluded from every send.

BFCM26 · Wave 1 · Engaged 180d

Opened or clicked an email in the last 180 days, or placed an order in the last 365 days. Can receive email.

BFCM26 · Wave 2 · Engaged 181–365d

Opened or clicked in the last 365 days, and zero times in the last 180.

BFCM26 · Wave 3 · Engaged 1–2 yrs

Opened or clicked in the last 730 days, and zero times in the last 365. Email 1 only, and only if waves 1 and 2 stay inside the guardrails.

Do not mail · Never engaged

No opens or clicks in 2 years. Leave them out of BFCM, then run a sunset email in January.

BFCM26 · Early list

Clicked an email link containing early=bfcm26 since 11/17, or in the "VIP Customers List," or subscribed to SMS.

BFCM26 · Bought

Placed an order since Wed 11/25 at 6pm ET. Excluded from emails 5 and 6 and SMS 3 and 4.

Guardrails. After each wave of Email 1, check the spam complaint rate (keep it under 0.08%) and the bounce rate (under 1%). If either goes over, stop widening the list and send the sale only to waves already mailed.

Settings. Sender "Shay from The Healthy Hand" from info@thehealthyhand.com, with a reply inbox someone actually reads. Turn on UTM tracking (it was off for every send last year).

How we'll know it worked

Measure

Reactivation
Share of waves 2 and 3 who open or click Email 1. Early-list size by Wed 11/25.
Revenue
Total vs. last year's $745. Revenue per recipient and placed-order rate per send.
List health
Unsubscribes under 0.3% per send, spam under 0.08%. Clicks well above last year's 0.5%.

A/B test: Email 4 subject lines, "Your Thanksgiving plate, itemized" vs. "We saw that second plate." Send each to 20% of the audience, pick the winner by click rate after 2 hours, and send it to the other 60%.

Before anything gets built

Needs sign-off

  1. The offer. The plan assumes 40% off everything, no code needed, Wed 11/25 6pm (early) to Mon 11/30 11:59pm ET. Confirm the percentage and whether bundles get it too, since they're already marked down.
  2. How early access works. Either schedule the Shopify automatic discount for Wed 6pm without announcing it publicly, or give the early list a Shopify discount link that applies 40% when they click.
  3. Shay reads Email 1. It's in her voice and mentions her daughter and the herbs she grew up with. She should change anything that isn't quite her.
  4. A photo of Shay. Her in the kitchen with a mug of tea, natural light. Email 1 is built around it.
  5. The site and packaging claims. The emails stay within the claim rules, but they link to product pages and show packaging that say "flush belly fat fast," "eliminate parasites," and that the tea lowers blood pressure and "prevents cancer." Those are the claims that get supplement brands FTC letters. Worth a review before traffic goes there.
  6. Small facts to check: tea steep time (we wrote 5 minutes), capsule directions (2 daily), the $100 free-shipping threshold, and shipping times over the holiday.
  7. Approve the brand kit. Once it's approved, it can be saved to Klaviyo's brand library, and the emails can be built as drag-and-drop templates.
